⬡Undo & squash

A one-button escape hatch for "that wasn't what I meant", and a quick way to fold a commit into its parent.

Undo last operation undo.last

The toolbar express lane that does the right thing depending on repo state.

Entry points

  • ToolbarThe Undo button (right side, amber on hover).

What happens next

  1. If a merge / rebase / cherry-pick / revert is in progress, it aborts that operation.
  2. Otherwise it rewinds to ORIG_HEAD with reset --hard — the state before the last merge/rebase/reset/pull.
  3. A dirty tree is auto-stashed first, so nothing is lost.
